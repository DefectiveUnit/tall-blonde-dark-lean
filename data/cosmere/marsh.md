# Marsh (Ironeyes)

**Series:** Mistborn Era 1 (and Era 2)
**Source:** [Coppermind - Marsh](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Marsh)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Light blonde (similar to Kelsier — they're brothers)
- Eyes: Had spikes driven through them as a Steel Inquisitor
- Build: Broad-shouldered, statuesque rigidity
- Style: Modest clothing pre-Inquisitor. Steel spikes through the eyes post-conversion.

## Key Quotes
- "Tall and broad-shouldered, he had a statuesque rigidity"
- Looks "very similar to his brother Kelsier"
