# Vasher (Zahel)

**Series:** Warbreaker / The Stormlight Archive
**Source:** [Coppermind - Vasher](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Vasher)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Scruffy — perpetually looks unshaven in exactly the same way (because he's a Returned and his body doesn't change)
- Eyes: Not prominently described
- Build: Can alter his physique as a Returned; generally solid/large
- Notable: Carries Nightblood in a silver sheath. Wears simple wraps/clothing.

## Key Quotes
- "He doesn't really like people. He gets frustrated and snaps at them."
- "Rough, brutal, and has a terrible temper... but he is a good man"
- "There is no guile in him and he wears no false mask"
- Vivenna notes he looks like "he purposely shaves to look scruffy"
