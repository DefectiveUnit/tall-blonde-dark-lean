# Szeth-son-son-Vallano

**Series:** The Stormlight Archive
**Source:** [Coppermind - Szeth](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Szeth)

## Physical Description
- Height: Short (small frame, notably shorter than Alethi characters)
- Hair: Brown fuzz (keeps head shaved or near-shaved)
- Eyes: Dark green (pale sapphire when using Honorblade; grey-white when infused with Stormlight)
- Build: Short, compact
- Skin: Pale, ghostly — Shin features (round eyes, light skin, looks "childlike" by Alethi standards)
- Notable: Known as "the Assassin in White" for his white clothing

## Key Quotes
- "Pale, ghostly skin"
- "A short frame, and large round eyes"
- "His hair is short, a brown fuzz, as if he'd kept it shaved until recently"
