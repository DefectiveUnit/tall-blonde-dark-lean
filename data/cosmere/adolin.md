# Adolin Kholin

**Series:** The Stormlight Archive
**Source:** [Coppermind - Adolin](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Adolin_Kholin)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall (Alethi, Kholin family)
- Hair: Blond with black strands — the blond came from his mother (Evi, who was not Alethi)
- Eyes: Light blue
- Build: Broad and solid — "more dorito than Dalinar's brick wall", broader than Kaladin's lean frame
- Face: Handsome, captivating smile, "stylish mess" hair

## Key Quotes
- Hair described as a "stylish mess" / unruly mop
- Build compared: "broader and more solid than Kaladin's lean lankiness"
