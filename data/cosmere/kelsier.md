# Kelsier

**Series:** Mistborn Era 1 (and beyond)
**Source:** [Coppermind - Kelsier](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Kelsier)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Light blonde
- Eyes: Hazel
- Build: Lean, lanky (shares build with brother Marsh). Athletic rather than bulky.
- Face: Hawk-faced
- Notable: Multitude of scars crossing both arms from the Pits of Hathsin. Mid-thirties.

## Key Quotes
- "Tall and hawk-faced with light-blonde hair and hazel eyes"
- "Always smiling as an act of rebellion against the Lord Ruler's oppression"
- Described as able to "charm birds out of the trees"
