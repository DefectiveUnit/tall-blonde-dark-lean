# Dalinar Kholin

**Series:** The Stormlight Archive
**Source:** [Coppermind - Dalinar](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Dalinar_Kholin)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall (no exact measurement, but described as large/imposing)
- Hair: Black, short, silvering at the temples
- Eyes: Blue (lighteyes)
- Build: Powerfully built, large, muscular even in his 50s. "Can still wrestle trained athletes to the ground without Stormlight."
- Face: Square, warrior's face, "blunted features", clean-shaven. Not notably handsome compared to his brother Gavilar.

## Key Quotes
- Shallan observes: "a man with blunted features, and short black hair silvering at the sides"
- "Square face, the face of a warrior"
