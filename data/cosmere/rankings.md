## Notable Observations

- **Kelsier** tops the board largely because he's the only character who scores high on *both* blonde AND dark — the smiling revolutionary with a genocidal streak toward nobles.
- **Marsh** is the dark horse (literally). The Inquisitor transformation pushes his dark/tough/strong/mean scores through the roof.
- **Toughness** is the great equaliser — almost everyone in the Cosmere is tough. It's the easiest 5 to earn.
- **Mean** is the hardest to score on — Sanderson writes fundamentally good people. Nobody cracks a 5.
- **Adolin and Elend** are the "nice boys" of the Cosmere — scoring high on blonde/tall but cratering on rough/dark/mean.
- **Raoden** suffers from limited physical description data. Scores are low confidence.
