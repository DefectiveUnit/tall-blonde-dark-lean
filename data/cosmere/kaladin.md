# Kaladin Stormblessed

**Series:** The Stormlight Archive
**Source:** [Coppermind - Kaladin](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Kaladin)

## Physical Description
- Height: ~6'4" (only a few inches shorter than Rock, who is nearly 7 feet)
- Hair: Black, shoulder-length, wavy (Alethi)
- Eyes: Dark brown (pale glassy blue when summoning Syl)
- Build: Brawny soldier/bridgeman physique, muscular but described with "lean lankiness" compared to Adolin's broader frame
- Notable: Slave brands on forehead, often hidden by hair. Shash glyph brand.

## Key Quotes
- "He had a perpetual storm for an expression, an intensity that made men wilt when it turned on them"
- Described as having "the brawny physique of a soldier and a bridgeman"
