# Sazed (Harmony)

**Series:** Mistborn Era 1 (becomes Harmony in Era 2)
**Source:** [Coppermind - Sazed](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Sazed)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Not described (Terrisman)
- Eyes: Not prominently described
- Build: Tall and long-limbed, scholarly. Arms and legs slightly too long for his body (result of castration as a boy — Terris Keeper tradition under the Lord Ruler)
- Notable: Stretched earlobes with studs running around the perimeter (metalminds). Eunuch.

## Key Quotes
- "Tall and long-limbed, with the build of a scholar"
- "His arms and legs are a bit too long for his body"
