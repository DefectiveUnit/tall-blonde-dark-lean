# Hoid (Wit)

**Series:** Cosmere-wide (prominently in Stormlight Archive)
**Source:** [Coppermind - Hoid](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Hoid)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: White (true appearance)
- Eyes: Bright blue
- Build: Thin
- Face: Hawkish, sharp nose and jaw, delicate eyebrows
- Notable: Uses Lightweaving to change appearance. True form is tall, thin, lined features. Cocks his head to one side.

## Key Quotes
- "A tall individual with a hawkish face, sharp nose and jaw accompanied with delicate eyebrows with bright blue eyes"
- Personality: "sharp wit and surprising compassion" — appears during Kaladin's darkest moments
