# Xaden Riorson

**Series:** Fourth Wing / The Empyrean (Rebecca Yarros)
**Source:** [Empyrean Wiki - Xaden](https://the-empyrean-series.fandom.com/wiki/Xaden_Riorson)

## Physical Description
- Height: "Well over six feet"
- Hair: Tousled black
- Eyes: Onyx with flecks of gold
- Build: Impressively muscled, broad-shouldered
- Skin: Tawny-brown
- Notable: Scar bisecting left eyebrow. Rebellion relic (tattoo) curling from wrist to neck on left arm. 107 scars on his back for each child of the rebellion he took responsibility for.

## Key Quotes
- "Well over six feet tall, impressively muscled and broad-shouldered"
- Scar from Sgaeyl across eyebrow
