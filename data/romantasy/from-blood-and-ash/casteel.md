## Physical Description
- Height: Over 6', very tall
- Hair: Dark, thick, curling at the nape
- Eyes: Golden/honey
- Build: Large, powerful
- Skin: Not specified
- Notable: Dimples when smiling. Can show fangs. Over 200 years old, appears mid-twenties. Posed as "Hawke," a Royal Guard.
