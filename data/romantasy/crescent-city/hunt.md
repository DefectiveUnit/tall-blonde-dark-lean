# Hunt Athalar

**Series:** Crescent City (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [CC Wiki - Hunt](https://crescentcity.fandom.com/wiki/Hunt_Athalar)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall, very muscular
- Hair: Dark, shoulder-length (later cut shorter)
- Eyes: Dark brown, angular
- Build: Extremely muscular
- Skin: Light brown / golden-brown / tan
- Notable: Grey angel wings. Black thorn "halo" tattoo on forehead (former slave brand). "The Umbra Mortis" — the Shadow of Death.
