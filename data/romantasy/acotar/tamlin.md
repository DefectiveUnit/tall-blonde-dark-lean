# Tamlin

**Series:** A Court of Thorns and Roses (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [ACOTAR Wiki - Tamlin](https://acourtofthornsandroses.fandom.com/wiki/Tamlin)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Long golden blond
- Eyes: Deep green flecked with gold
- Build: Warrior's build, tanned, honed over centuries
- Notable: Can shapeshift. Often in beast form with claws and fangs.
