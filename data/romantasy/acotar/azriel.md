# Azriel

**Series:** A Court of Thorns and Roses (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [ACOTAR Wiki - Azriel](https://acourtofthornsandroses.fandom.com/wiki/Azriel)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall (Illyrian)
- Hair: Black
- Eyes: Hazel
- Build: Tall, lean-muscular, elegant planes
- Skin: Tanned
- Notable: Hands heavily scarred from being burnt as a child. Seven cobalt siphons. Wreathed in living shadows that obey him. "Classically beautiful but near-unreadable."
