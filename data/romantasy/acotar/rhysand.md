# Rhysand

**Series:** A Court of Thorns and Roses (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [ACOTAR Wiki - Rhysand](https://acourtofthornsandroses.fandom.com/wiki/Rhysand)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall (Illyrian, imposing)
- Hair: Black, short
- Eyes: Violet
- Build: Muscular, tan/golden skin, Illyrian wings (usually glamoured)
- Notable: Extensive tattoos — arms, shoulders, pectorals, spine, knees (mountain crowned with three stars)
