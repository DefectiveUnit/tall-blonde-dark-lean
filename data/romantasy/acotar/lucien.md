# Lucien Vanserra

**Series:** A Court of Thorns and Roses (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [ACOTAR Wiki - Lucien](https://acourtofthornsandroses.fandom.com/wiki/Lucien_Vanserra)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall (Fae)
- Hair: Long, fire-red
- Eyes: One russet, one gold mechanical eye (replaced after Amarantha ripped it out)
- Build: Lean, not as heavily muscled as the Illyrians
- Notable: Metal eye is a distinctive feature. Fox-like cunning.
