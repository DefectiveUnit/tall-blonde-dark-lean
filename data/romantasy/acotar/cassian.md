# Cassian

**Series:** A Court of Thorns and Roses (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [ACOTAR Wiki - Cassian](https://acourtofthornsandroses.fandom.com/wiki/Cassian)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tallest of the Illyrian brothers (well over 6')
- Hair: Black, shoulder-length, unruly
- Eyes: Hazel/golden-brown
- Build: Massive, broad-shouldered, "built like a warlord." Most muscular of the bat boys.
- Skin: Golden-tan
- Notable: Illyrian wings, seven red siphons, "rough-hewn features"
