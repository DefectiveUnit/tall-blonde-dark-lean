## Notable Observations

- **Rowan Whitethorn** takes the crown — silver hair (scoring on blonde), centuries of warrior darkness, and raw power push him to 31.
- **Romantasy skews dark and tough** — the genre's obsession with brooding immortal warriors means almost everyone scores 4+ on dark and tough.
- **Blonde is extremely rare** — only Tamlin, Fenrys, and Aedion have genuinely light hair. SJM apparently has a type (dark-haired Fae warriors).
- **Lean is the dump stat** — romantasy males are universally built like trucks. Dorian is the only lean character at 5.
- **Cassian and Lucien** suffer from the "too nice" penalty — warm, loyal, kind hearts score poorly on dark/mean.
- **Mean barely exists** — romantasy males are protective, not cruel. Even the "dark" ones are secretly devoted. Lorcan and Tamlin are the meanest, and even they top out at 4.
