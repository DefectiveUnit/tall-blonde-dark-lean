# Dorian Havilliard

**Series:** Throne of Glass (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [TOG Wiki - Dorian](https://throneofglass.fandom.com/wiki/Dorian_Havilliard)

## Physical Description
- Height: 6'0"
- Hair: Raven-black
- Eyes: Sapphire blue ("the color of the waters of the southern countries")
- Build: Tall, lean
- Notable: Crown prince. Handsome, well-groomed. Dark eyebrows.
