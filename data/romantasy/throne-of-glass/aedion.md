# Aedion Ashryver

**Series:** Throne of Glass (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [TOG Wiki - Aedion](https://throneofglass.fandom.com/wiki/Aedion_Ashryver)

## Physical Description
- Height: 6'3"
- Hair: Long, golden blond
- Eyes: Turquoise rimmed with gold (Ashryver eyes)
- Build: Heavily muscled, sun-kissed complexion
- Notable: Half-Fae. Known as "Adarlan's Whore" for pretending to serve the king while secretly building resistance.
