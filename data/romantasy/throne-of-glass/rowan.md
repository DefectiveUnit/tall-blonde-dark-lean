# Rowan Whitethorn

**Series:** Throne of Glass (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [TOG Wiki - Rowan](https://throneofglass.fandom.com/wiki/Rowan_Whitethorn)

## Physical Description
- Height: 6'4", broad-shouldered
- Hair: Silver (later cut short for combat)
- Eyes: Pine-green
- Build: "Every inch corded with muscle." Broad-shouldered, massive.
- Skin: Tan
- Notable: Wicked tattoo from temple to jaw to throat. Fae with slightly elongated canines and arched ears. Hawk shifter.
