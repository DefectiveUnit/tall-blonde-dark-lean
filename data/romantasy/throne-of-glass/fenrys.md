# Fenrys

**Series:** Throne of Glass (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [TOG Wiki - Fenrys](https://throneofglass.fandom.com/wiki/Fenrys)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall (Fae)
- Hair: Long, wavy blonde
- Eyes: Onyx black
- Build: Muscular, warrior
- Skin: Bronze
- Notable: "Incredibly beautiful male." White wolf shifter (twin brother shifts to black wolf). Maeve's blood-sworn.
