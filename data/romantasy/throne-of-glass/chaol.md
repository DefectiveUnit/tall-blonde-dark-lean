# Chaol Westfall

**Series:** Throne of Glass (Sarah J. Maas)
**Source:** [TOG Wiki - Chaol](https://throneofglass.fandom.com/wiki/Chaol_Westfall)

## Physical Description
- Height: 6'1"
- Hair: Chestnut/bronze, close-cropped
- Eyes: Copper-brown
- Build: Muscular, tall. "Handsome in a rugged way."
- Notable: Paralysed from waist down after events of Queen of Shadows. Later healed partially.
