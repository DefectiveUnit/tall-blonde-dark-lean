# Rubeus Hagrid

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Rubeus Hagrid](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Rubeus_Hagrid)

## Physical Description
- Height: Half-giant. Approximately 8'6" — twice the height of a normal man
- Hair: Thick, wild black hair and a tangled black beard
- Eyes: Black, described as "beetle-like" and "glinting like black beetles"
- Build: Enormous. "Almost twice as tall as a normal man and at least five times as wide"
- Notable: Hands "the size of dustbin lids." Wears a massive moleskin overcoat.

## Key Quotes
- "Almost twice as tall as a normal man and at least five times as wide"
- Hands "the size of dustbin lids", feet "like baby dolphins"
