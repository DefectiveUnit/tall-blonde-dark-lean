# Tom Riddle / Lord Voldemort

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Tom Riddle](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Tom_Riddle)

## Physical Description
- **Young Tom Riddle:** Tall, handsome, pale skin, jet-black hair, dark brown eyes. Charming and beautiful.
- **Voldemort (post-rebirth):** Tall, skeletally thin. "Whiter than a skull, with wide, livid scarlet eyes and a nose as flat as a snake's with slits for nostrils." Spidery fingers, high cold voice.
- Notable: Progressively lost his looks as he split his soul. The transformation from handsome young man to snake-faced horror is central to his arc.

## Key Quotes
- Young Riddle: "Tall, handsome, with pale skin, jet-black hair, and dark brown eyes"
- Voldemort: "Tall and skeletally thin... whiter than a skull, with wide, livid scarlet eyes"
