# Albus Dumbledore

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Albus Dumbledore](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Albus_Dumbledore)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Silver (auburn in youth), long enough to tuck into belt
- Eyes: Blue, "light, bright, and sparkling behind half-moon spectacles"
- Build: Tall, thin
- Face: Very long, crooked nose (broken at least twice). Long silver beard.
- Notable: Wears flamboyant robes — purple, midnight blue, covered in stars and moons.

## Key Quotes
- "Tall, thin, and very old, judging by the silver of his hair and beard, which were both long enough to tuck into his belt"
- "His blue eyes were light, bright, and sparkling behind half-moon spectacles"
