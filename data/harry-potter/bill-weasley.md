# Bill Weasley

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Lexicon - Bill Weasley](https://www.hp-lexicon.org/character/weasley-family/bill-weasley/)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Long red hair tied in a ponytail
- Eyes: Not specified
- Build: Tall and slim
- Face: Scarred by Fenrir Greyback (later books)
- Notable: Fang earring, "dresses as though for a rock concert." Cool older brother archetype. Curse-breaker for Gringotts.

## Key Quotes
- "Tall, with long hair tied back in a ponytail"
- "Dressed as though for a rock concert"
- Molly disapproves of the long hair and earring
