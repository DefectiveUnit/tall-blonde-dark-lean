# Remus Lupin

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Remus Lupin](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Remus_Lupin)

## Physical Description
- Height: Not specified as notably tall or short
- Hair: Light brown, becoming increasingly grey prematurely
- Eyes: Not prominently described
- Build: Thin, worn, frail-looking. "Looked as if one good curse would finish him off"
- Face: Pale, premature lines, scars. Thin moustache, later a rough beard.
- Notable: Shabby, patched clothing. Looks older than his age due to werewolf transformations.

## Key Quotes
- "Looked as if one good curse would be able to finish him off"
- Pale face with premature lines, light brown hair going grey
