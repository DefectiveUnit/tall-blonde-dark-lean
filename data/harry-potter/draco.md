# Draco Malfoy

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Draco Malfoy](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Draco_Malfoy)

## Physical Description
- Height: Slightly taller than Harry (~6'1")
- Hair: Sleek white-blond
- Eyes: Cold grey
- Build: Slender
- Face: Pale complexion, sharp pointed features
- Notable: Always impeccably dressed. Pale, aristocratic look.

## Key Quotes
- "Slender with sleek white-blond hair, cold grey eyes, a pale complexion and rather sharp, pointed features"
- Harry's first impression: "a boy with a pale, pointed face"
