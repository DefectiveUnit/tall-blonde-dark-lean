# Fred & George Weasley

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Fred and George](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Fred_and_George_Weasley)

## Physical Description
- Height: Shorter, stockier side (like Charlie, not like Ron/Bill)
- Hair: Red (Weasley trademark)
- Eyes: Not specified
- Build: Short, stocky, broad
- Face: Freckled. Fred rounder-faced, George more oval with a nose bump
- Notable: Identical twins. George loses an ear (Sectumsempra from Snape). Always finishing each other's sentences.

## Key Quotes
- Described as shorter and stockier, like Charlie
- "Short, stocky, with freckles"
