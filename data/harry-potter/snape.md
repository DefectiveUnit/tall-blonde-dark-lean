# Severus Snape

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Severus Snape](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Severus_Snape)

## Physical Description
- Height: Not explicitly stated but implied tall (looming presence)
- Hair: Greasy, black, shoulder-length, "hung in curtains around his face"
- Eyes: Cold, black
- Build: Thin
- Face: Sallow skin, large hooked nose, yellow uneven teeth
- Notable: Wears black flowing robes — described as looking like "an overgrown bat"

## Key Quotes
- "A thin man with sallow skin, a large, hooked nose, and greasy black hair"
- Appearance of "an overgrown bat"
- Cold black eyes that are "sometimes glittery"
