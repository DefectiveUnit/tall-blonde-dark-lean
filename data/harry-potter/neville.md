# Neville Longbottom

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Neville Longbottom](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Neville_Longbottom)

## Physical Description
- Height: Short (JKR described him as "short and plump")
- Hair: Blond/sandy, darkening to brown over time
- Eyes: Not prominently described
- Build: Round-faced, plump as a child. Grows taller and thinner by later books.
- Notable: Clumsy, forgetful. Transforms from bumbling boy to resistance leader.

## Key Quotes
- JKR: "short and plump and blond"
- "A round-faced boy"
