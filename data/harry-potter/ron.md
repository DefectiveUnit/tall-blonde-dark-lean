# Ron Weasley

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Ron Weasley](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Ronald_Weasley)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Flaming red (Weasley trademark)
- Eyes: Blue
- Build: Thin, gangling
- Face: Long nose, freckles, big hands and feet
- Notable: "Tall, thin and gangling, with freckles, big hands and feet, and a long nose"

## Key Quotes
- "Tall, thin and gangling, with freckles, big hands and feet, and a long nose"

## Scores

| Dimension | Score | Justification |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| **Tall** | 5 | Explicitly tall and gangling. One of the taller characters. |
| **Blonde** | 2 | Red hair, not blonde. Weasley ginger is distinctive but not remotely blonde. A point above jet-black for being light-ish. |
| **Dark** | 1 | The least dark major character in the series. Comic relief, loyal friend, insecure but warm. His darkest moment is briefly leaving during the Horcrux hunt, and he came back. |
| **Lean** | 4 | Thin and gangling. All limbs and freckles. |
| **Rough** | 3 | Grew up poor in a chaotic household. Table manners are questionable. Not refined. But he's also fundamentally gentle and domesticated — his mum knits him jumpers. |
| **Tough** | 3 | Fought in the Battle of Hogwarts, destroyed a Horcrux, played through a chess game knowing he'd be sacrificed at age 11. But also the most emotionally fragile of the trio — jealousy, insecurity, the locket breaking him. |
| **Strong** | 2 | Average wizard. Not notably powerful. Good at chess. His strengths are loyalty and heart, not raw magical ability. |
| **Mean** | 1 | Kind-hearted, loyal to a fault. His insults are clumsy rather than cutting. Would never deliberately hurt anyone he cares about. |
