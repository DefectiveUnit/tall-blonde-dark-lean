# Sirius Black

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Sirius Black](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Sirius_Black)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Long, dark/black
- Eyes: Grey
- Build: Pre-Azkaban: handsome, well-built. Post-Azkaban: gaunt, wasted
- Notable: Described as "carelessly handsome" in his youth with a "slightly spoiled, haughty look". After 12 years in Azkaban: gaunt face, dirty hair, yellow teeth.

## Key Quotes
- "Carelessly handsome" (pre-Azkaban)
- Harry sees him in the Pensieve as a teenager: tall, handsome, with an air of casual elegance
