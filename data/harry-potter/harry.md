# Harry Potter

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Harry Potter](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Harry_Potter)

## Physical Description
- Height: "Small and skinny" as a boy; grows to match James who was tall
- Hair: Untidy jet-black, sticking up at the back
- Eyes: "Startlingly green" (almond-shaped, like Lily's)
- Build: Thin, wiry. Malnourished childhood under the Dursleys.
- Face: Thin face, lightning bolt scar, round glasses
- Notable: "The boy who lived." Constantly told he looks like James but has his mother's eyes.

## Key Quotes
- "Small and skinny for his age" with "a thin face, knobbly knees, black hair and bright-green eyes"
- "He wore round glasses held together with a lot of Sellotape"
