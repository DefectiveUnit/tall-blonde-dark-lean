# Cedric Diggory

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - Cedric Diggory](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Cedric_Diggory)

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall
- Hair: Dark
- Eyes: Grey
- Build: Well-built, burly despite being a Seeker
- Face: "Exceptionally handsome", chiselled features, straight nose
- Notable: Angelina: "tall and good-looking". Katie: "strong and silent".

## Key Quotes
- "Exceptionally handsome, with his straight nose, dark hair, and grey eyes"
- "Tall and extremely handsome young man with chiselled features"
