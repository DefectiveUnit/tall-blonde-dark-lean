# James Potter

**Series:** Harry Potter
**Source:** [Harry Potter Wiki - James Potter](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/James_Potter_(I))

## Physical Description
- Height: Tall (Harry grows to match him; described as tall)
- Hair: Black, untidy, sticking up at the back (like Harry's)
- Eyes: Hazel (behind glasses)
- Build: Tall and thin
- Face: Longer nose than Harry, thin face, same mouth and eyebrows
- Notable: Animagus (stag). Same messy-hair-and-glasses look as Harry.

## Key Quotes
- "Tall and thin" with "untidy black hair which stood up at the back"
- Same height as Harry when both were 17
