## Notable Observations

- **Voldemort and Snape** dominate because villainy scores extremely well on this rubric. Dark, lean, tough, mean — evil is a great look for these lyrics.
- **Sirius Black** is the highest-scoring "good guy" — his name, Animagus, Azkaban stint, and rebellious streak all play perfectly.
- **Cedric Diggory** finishes dead last (phrasing intended). Being handsome, fair, and decent scores terribly here — he's the anti-thesis of every dimension except height.
- **Hagrid** has the most extreme profile — 5/5 on tall/rough/tough/strong but 1/1/1/1 on blonde/dark/lean/mean.
- **Toughness** is again the great equaliser — almost everyone scores high because Rowling put her characters through hell.
- **Blonde** is even harder to score in HP than the Cosmere — almost everyone has dark hair except Draco and Neville.
